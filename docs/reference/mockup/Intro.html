INTRO, ABOUT 3 SECONDS

Plays once per session. Skips on click, any key or the skip button. Does not play under reduced motion.
skip
1. Desk (0.0 s)Cream fills the screen. Stitch lines sew in from opposite sides. The skip button is there from the first frame.
2. Keys drop (0.4 s)Keycaps tumble in from the top, each with its own spin, and bounce once like dropped paper.
3. Keys build the name (1.4 s)The keys slide together and flip over to become the six letters. A mint burst pops as the last one lands.
4. Beaver, then the page (2.2 s)The plaque drops under the name and the beaver pops up. The wordmark settles into its place in the hero and the rest of the page is uncovered.
